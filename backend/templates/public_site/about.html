Our Story

A garden retreat in the heart of Eldoret

Since 2010, Queens Garden Hotel has been the place travelers to Eldoret turn to for a warm welcome and a comfortable stay. Just off Kisumu Road, our gardens, restaurant, and rooms were built around one simple idea, that a guest should feel at home the moment they walk through our gates.

That idea still shapes everything we do today, from the meals we serve to the way our staff greet every guest by name.

Meet the Founder

Hon. Joseph Wainaina Iraya

Hon. Joseph Wainaina Iraya's journey to becoming one of Eldoret's most respected hoteliers did not start in hospitality at all. He rose from humble beginnings, once working as a taxi driver in the North Rift, and built that early hustle into real entrepreneurship. That drive eventually led him to found Queens Garden Hotel, a home away from home for everyone who walks through its doors.

Beyond the hotel, Hon. Wainaina is well known across Uasin Gishu County for his work bringing communities together, especially in the years following the 2007 to 2008 post election period. That work in peace and reconciliation earned him an honorary doctorate in December 2023.
